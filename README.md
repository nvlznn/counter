Privacy Policy — Counters

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Counters does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Your counters are stored locally on your device and, if iCloud is enabled, in your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. This data is only accessible to you. The developer has no access to it.

Counters does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or any third-party services.
